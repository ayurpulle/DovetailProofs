\small
\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.6em,style=unboxed,itemsep=4pt]

\item[(1)--(3)] Histories are identified when they have the same
conditional law over every finite future, and impossible extensions go
to one absorbing dead state.  Mapping a predictor's state to the future
law it predicts is the refinement familiar from computational
mechanics.

\item[(4)] Intertwining descends from states to generated
transformations.  The proof uses only that $L$ is onto and commutes
with the updates.

\item[(5)] Computing predictive states and multiplying in $S_p$ are the
same task, which is what lets circuit complexity of word problems
apply.

\end{description}

\noindent\textbf{What is new.}
The passage from a stochastic process to its monoid, the proof that the
monoid is forced on every exact predictor, and the transport of
subgroups, aperiodicity, group complexity and circuit class.  Predictive
states, $\varepsilon$-transducers, Hankel minimisation, syntactic
monoids and the complexity of word problems are prior work.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{What the invariants do not see.}
All of them read $S_p$ alone.  Z1R and RRXOR have $S_p$ determined by
the pattern of possible words; a full-support Markov chain has all of
its structure in the likelihoods.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{The congruence lattice.}
$\Con(S_p)$ is inherited as the interval $[\ker\Phi,\top]$ of
$\Con(S_A)$.  A first-order Markov chain with positive, distinct rows
has $S_p\cong R_n^1$ and $\Con(S_p)\cong\Pi_n\oplus\mathbf 1$, which
contains $M_3$ for $n\ge3$, while its predictor only stores the last
symbol.  Non-distributivity therefore certifies nothing about internal
features.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Control.}
Reduced to a remark: a controller inherits the structure when recovery
of the environment is state-local, and optimality does not supply that.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Experiment.}
Small transformers trained to predict processes chosen by the class of
$S_p$: what they represent, and whether they keep predicting beyond the
trained length (Section ``An experiment'').

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Machine-checked.}
The monoid-level results are formalised in Lean in
\texttt{DovetailProofs/Predictor}.  The complexity theorem and the
transformer corollary are not.
